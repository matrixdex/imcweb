Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 2026

In short: this website does not use cookies, does not track you across other sites, and does not run advertising or analytics scripts that identify you. The only information we collect is what you choose to give us — for example, by emailing or calling us directly.

Who We Are

This website is operated by Integrated Medical Centre ("we", "us", "our"), an integrated medical practice based at 75 Harley Street, London, W1G 8QL, United Kingdom.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, you can reach us at enquiries@theintegratedmed.com or by telephone on +44 207-224-5111.

This Website Uses No Cookies

We do not use cookies, browser local storage, tracking pixels, or any similar technology to identify or follow visitors on this website. We do not run third-party advertising networks or analytics platforms that track individual visitors. As a result, this site does not display a cookie consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to.

Information We Collect

We only collect information that you choose to provide to us directly. This may include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address, if you contact us to enquire about an appointment or consultation
  • Details you choose to share about your health concerns, preferred appointment times or other information relevant to your enquiry
  • Any information you send us directly by email or telephone

We do not automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, or browsing behaviour through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or booking request
  • Arrange consultations or treatments you have requested
  • Communicate with you about your appointment, order or ongoing care where relevant

We do not use your information for marketing purposes without your explicit consent, and we do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with any third party, except where necessary to provide the care or service you have requested (for example, with a consultant or partner facility involved in your treatment), or where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry and correspondence information only for as long as is reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, provide the service requested, or comply with our legal and accounting obligations. You may ask us to delete information we hold about you at any time, as set out below.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law (including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), you have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct any inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Object to or restrict how we use your information
  • Ask for a copy of your information in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details above. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children's Privacy

This website and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for legal reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at enquiries@theintegratedmed.com or +44 207-224-5111.
