Children's Clinic

Mayfair Children's Clinic is our dedicated outpatient clinic for children. Children from infants to young adults receive outstanding integrated care in a calm, friendly environment.

Book appointment

Child care specialists

At Mayfair Children's Clinic, our highly experienced consultants are among the very best in London. We are trusted by parents from all over the UK and beyond. Our standard is personalised, high-quality care for children and young people aged 0-18. Founded on the conviction that children deserve care as unique as they are, we empower families to prioritise their children's health and well-being through exceptional paediatric care in an environment that fosters connection and quality.

Balanced approach

We follow an integrated philosophy: treat the whole child, not just their symptoms. We strive to understand the root cause of illness. Our blend of conventional medicine with gentle, natural therapies is balanced care for the young. Our care is personalised and delivered in a calm, child-friendly environment.

Early and holistic intervention is the foundation of lifelong health. Every treatment plan is carefully crafted to support growth and development.
