Meet Dr Ali

Integrated Medical Centre

Under the leadership of Dr. Imran Ali, the Integrated Medical Centre (IMC) has expanded beyond its original roots, making a significant impact across the globe as a pioneer in Integrated Medicine. Dr. Ali was instrumental in establishing the Integrated Medical Centre in London's prestigious medical district, where he introduced a range of innovative treatments that have set new standards in evidence-based healthcare. This expansion was driven by his vision to merge the best practices from conventional, complementary, and traditional medicine, creating a unique healing environment.

By assembling a diverse team of top-tier specialists, Dr. Ali ensured that each patient's care was personalised and deeply rooted in a complete understanding of health and wellness.

Our Philosophy

At IMC, we are dedicated to treat the whole person - not just their symptoms and signs - by addressing root causes and empowering our patients throughout their health journey. Our unique approach merges evidence-based medicine with natural treatment, emphasising preventative care and crafting personalised treatment plans tailored to meet each individual's specific needs. This comprehensive methodology ensures that every aspect of our patients' well-being is considered and nurtured.

IMC is now located at 75 Harley Street, London and led by Dr. Imran Ali.

Embracing Innovation

In line with global trends and advancements, IMC has embraced digitalisation to enhance patient care. Our state-of-the-art facility integrates the latest technologies to deliver innovative treatments, prioritising both safety and comfort. By adopting these advancements, we ensure that our patients receive the most effective and efficient care available.

Our commitment to integrating modern technology reflects our dedication to providing exceptional healthcare tailored to the evolving needs of our patients.

What We Offer

1

Integrated Medicine

Conventional and complementary medicines under one roof. Acupuncture, nutrition, naturopathy and osteopathy relieve pain fast, prevent illness and manage chronic conditions.

2

Neuro-Muscular Rehabilitation

Restores smooth communication between nerves and muscles. Targeted exercises and retraining improves balance, coordination, strength and movement control. Effective in injury, surgery and neurological conditions.

3

Health Screening

Medical tests and evaluations for early detection. Timely check ups prevent long-term illness with early interventions.

4

Joint Injection

Targeted medication injected into an inflamed joint to relieve pain, reduce swelling and improve mobility. Quick, localized relief for arthritis or sports injuries.

5

Psychiatry

Medical science and psychological care combined in therapies and targeted medications to restore mental balance.

6

Ultrasound

Safe, non-invasive imaging technique for real-time internal views. We offer on-site ultrasounds at our partner facility.

7

Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)

Detailed, cross-sectional images of your organs and tissues. High precision diagnostics for joint, brain and soft tissue conditions without radiation. We offer on-site MRI at our partner facility.

8

Prescription

We work with your primary healthcare provider and current medication to offer prescriptions as needed.
