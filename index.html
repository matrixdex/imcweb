Who We Are

75 Harley Street, London

Integrated Medical Centre is an alternative centre and store, providing natural therapies such as osteopathy, acupuncture, hypoxic training, herbal vitamins and supplements. IMC is a drug-free, non-invasive clinic with the ultimate goal of health improvement across all bodily systems and was established in by Dr. Imran Ali.

Our treatments target the neurological, circulatory, and lymphatic systems with a focus on the joints, muscles, and spine. Our non-invasive care and emphasis on manual diagnosis and treatment sets us apart from other medical practices. We do, however, provide additional services related to cosmetic procedures and physiotherapy, as well as cutting-edge technology Hypoxic training machines with the goal of improving blood circulation (especially in the brain), boosting your immune system, assisting with recovery after injuries and respiratory infections, decreasing inflammation, and much more.

Rehabilitation Clinic

Altitude training, also known as hypoxic training or low oxygen training, involves exercising in, living in or otherwise breathing oxygen reduced air. This is done to improve physical wellness and athletic performance. Training under a state of hypoxia can also help people acclimatise to altitude.

In the COVID-19 pandemic, South America and Tibet have demonstrated lower COVID-19 transmission due to the low oxygen environment.

3

Women's Health Clinic

The Integrated Medical Centre treats even more women than men and recognises why women need their own health programme — gynaecologists and obstetricians cannot understand all the problems of women, says Dr. Ali, so the clinic offers a pioneering approach to help women understand their specific health issues and deal with them themselves.

A woman's mind and body function differently from a man's and there are many different factors that contribute, including body types and birth signs.
