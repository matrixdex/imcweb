Meet Dr Ali

Integrated Medical Centre

In the beginning, Dr. A. Hameed integrated traditional Unani medicine and natural opiates for pain management. His daughter Ambia Khatun cared for the poor and needy compassionately. Her son Dr. Imran Ali integrates modern medicine with a historic and natural approach.

3 generations of the Ali family practice this evolving treatise of medicine, the Ali Technique.

British Legacy of Integrated Medicine

Continuing our heritage of integrating Eastern and Western medicines, IMC was estalished in Delhi and London in 1990. New-age osteopathy and neuro-muscular rehabilitation techniques combined with age-old natural formulations bring the best of medicine throughout the ages, to London's prestigious medical district, Harley Street.

IMC is now located at 75 Harley Street, London and led by Dr. Imran Ali.

Ethical Medicine that Works

Ethical medicine is the core of Dr. Imran Ali's vision for IMC. Patients are counselled on effective treatments keeping their conditions into account. Fast effective treatments are the reason for our continued existence in the health and wellness space for over 30 years.

Three generations on, IMC today combines the cutting-edge with time-tested natural healing.

What We Offer

1

Integrated Medicine

Conventional and complementary medicines under one roof. Acupuncture, nutrition, naturopathy and osteopathy relieve pain fast, prevent illness and manage chronic conditions.

2

Neuro-Muscular Rehabilitation

Restores smooth communication between nerves and muscles. Targeted exercises and retraining improves balance, coordination, strength and movement control. Effective in injury, surgery and neurological conditions.

3

Health Screening

Medical tests and evaluations for early detection. Timely check ups prevent long-term illness with early interventions.

4

Joint Injection

Targeted medication injected into an inflamed joint to relieve pain, reduce swelling and improve mobility. Quick, localized relief for arthritis or sports injuries.

5

Psychiatry

Medical science and psychological care combined in therapies and targeted medications to restore mental balance.

6

Ultrasound

Safe, non-invasive imaging technique for real-time internal views. We offer on-site ultrasounds at our partner facility.

7

Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)

Detailed, cross-sectional images of your organs and tissues. High precision diagnostics for joint, brain and soft tissue conditions without radiation. We offer on-site MRI at our partner facility.

8

Prescription

We work with your primary healthcare provider and current medication to offer prescriptions as needed.
