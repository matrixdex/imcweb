What We Offer

1

Integrated Medicine

We integrate conventional medicine with evidence-based complementary therapies—like acupuncture, nutrition, and naturopathy—to deliver personalized, patient-focused care. Our collaborative approach supports your physical, psychological, and emotional wellbeing, helping prevent illness and effectively manage chronic conditions.

2

Neuro Muscular Rehabilitation

Neuromuscular rehabilitation is a specialized therapy designed to restore smooth communication between your nerves and muscles. Through targeted exercises and retraining, it improves balance, coordination, strength, and movement control following injury, surgery, or neurological conditions to help you move safely.

3

Health Screening

Health screening involves proactive medical tests and evaluations designed to detect potential health issues or disease risks early—often before symptoms appear. This enables timely, personalized interventions to prevent illness, protect long-term wellness, and effectively manage your ongoing health.

4

Joint Injection

Joint injection therapy delivers targeted medication—such as corticosteroids or hyaluronic acid—directly into an inflamed joint to relieve pain, reduce swelling, and improve mobility. It provides quick, localized relief for chronic conditions like arthritis or sports injuries.

5

Psychiatry

Psychiatry is a medical specialty focused on diagnosing, treating, and preventing mental, emotional, and behavioral disorders. Combining medical science with psychological care, it uses therapies and targeted medications to restore mental balance, improve well-being, and support holistic recovery.

6

Ultrasound

Ultrasound is a safe, non-invasive imaging technique that uses high-frequency sound waves to create real-time visuals of your internal organs, tissues, and blood flow. It helps clinicians accurately diagnose conditions, monitor health, and guide targeted medical treatments.

7

MRI

Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) is a non-invasive scan using strong magnetic fields and radio waves to generate detailed, cross-sectional images of your organs and tissues. It offers high-precision diagnostics for joint, brain, and soft-tissue conditions without radiation.

8

Prescription

Prescription services provide fast, seamless access to your essential medications through expert clinical review and precise dispensing. Working closely with your healthcare providers, we ensure safe usage, accurate dosages, clear guidance, and convenient fulfillment tailored to your ongoing health needs.
